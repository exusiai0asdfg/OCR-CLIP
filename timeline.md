### ImageNet

- Compared several coordinate transformation methods against ImageNet localization annotations.

### Web Images

- Tried downloading images from the original URLs.
- Codex initially failed to access the official Google Drive archive and reported a 404 error.
- Successfully downloaded `from_web.tgz` manually through the browser.
- Extracted 123 required images from the official archive.
- Tried Wayback Machine, Common Crawl and other URL recovery methods.
- Recovered 10 images from historical Wayback snapshots in total.
- Used a local HTML tool generated by Codex to manually inspect the remaining failed URLs.
- Manually recovered another 31 images.

An interesting case:

宜宾新闻的原始图片 URL：

http://www.ybxww.com/content/2012-10/8/xinhua_sc_yibin20121081011208807032.jpg

直接访问会跳转到宜宾新闻首页。经过查询发现 `past.ybxww.com` 是用于存放旧新闻的子域名，于是将 `www` 替换为 `past`，成功获得原始图片。

This was an interesting example of recovering a missing image by investigating the website's migration instead of relying only on web archives.
